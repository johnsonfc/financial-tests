## 3. Margin-recovery drivers — Q3 FY26 call/transcripts (2026-05-05)
- CFO Weigand: Q2 compression was "heavy one-time expedite charges from a large
  December deployment, which did not recur in Q3 and are not expected to repeat
  at that scale."
- CEO Liang: DCBBS margins "consistently run above 20%, well above the company's
  hardware average"; DCBBS expected >25% of profit.
- Software bookings >$46M in Q3 FY26.
- Q3 drivers: improved customer/product mix; reduced tariffs/expedite/inventory
  reserve charges. Revenue miss = "component shortages and customer site
  readiness delays," not demand failure.

## 5. Securities class actions
- Lead case: Bhuva v. Super Micro Computer, Inc., No. 26-cv-02606 (N.D. Cal.).
- Related: Chung v. SMCI (Hagens Berman, filed 2026-05-12; does not move May 26 deadline).
- Class period: 2024-04-30 → 2026-03-19 (indictment = corrective-disclosure end).
- Defendants: SMCI, CEO Charles Liang, CFO David Weigand.
- Allegation: failure to disclose (i) significant server sales to China-based
  companies, (ii) export-control violations, (iii) material compliance weaknesses.
- Lead plaintiff deadline: 2026-05-26. Firms: Robbins Geller, Rosen, Hagens
  Berman, Frank R. Cruz, Kahn Swick & Foti.

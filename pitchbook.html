Thesis 2 — Margin Recovery (Already Inflected)

SMCI | Reinforced Thesis | May 19, 2026

The Margin V-Recovery — gross margin %, GAAP (navy) vs Non-GAAP (gold)

Source: SMCI 8-K Ex 99.1 (2026-05-05); Q4 GAAP guide per Motley Fool transcript 2026-05-05; FY25/Q1 FY26 per source report.
Mix-shift driver DCBBS margins >20% (CEO Liang), expected >25% of profit. Software bookings $46M in Q3 FY26 alone. Structural, recurring.
One-time reversal Q2 compression = "heavy one-time expedite charges from a large December deployment" (CFO Weigand). Did not recur in Q3.
Manufacturing leverage Lower expedite + tariff costs vs Q2; improved customer/product mix; reduced inventory-reserve charges.
Counterargument A single-quarter recovery is not durability proof. Dell + HPE entering DLC; industry-equilibrium GM may be permanently sub-10% as scale players compete.
Response Q4 GAAP guide 8.2–8.4% implies sustained recovery, not a blip. Mix math: each ~5pp shift toward DCBBS (~20% GM) from hardware (~9%) ≈ +55bp blended GM. The mix is the structural story, not the cycle.
Thesis 2 falsified if: (a) Q4 FY26 non-GAAP GM below 9%; (b) DCBBS attach stalls below 25% of revenue; (c) Dell or HPE ships a DLC product that closes the time-to-market gap.

Thesis 3 — Governance Discount (Honestly Reframed)

SMCI | Reinforced Thesis | May 19, 2026

Governance Timeline — Jan 2024 → Dec 2026

Sources: NatLawReview (Mar 2026 indictment); Investing.com (Apr 7 probe); rgrdlaw.com (Bhuva 26-cv-02606). All accessed 2026-05-19.
Material update since Feb report The original thesis hoped "DOJ closes with no charges." DOJ acted Mar 19 — that scenario is closed, partially:
  • SMCI NOT charged / not a defendant (partial bull)
  • 3 indicted incl. co-founder/board director Liaw (partial bear)
  • ~$2.5B alleged China diversion; Liaw resigned post-indictment
  • Conduct differs from 2024 review (Russia, board cleared)
Reframed thesis The discount existed for real reasons; the worst tail (corporate indictment) did NOT materialize. The discount may be overdone — but closure is now multi-stage, not binary:
  • (1) class action consolidated complaint → MTD ruling
  • (2) independent board investigation conclusion
  • (3) SEC investigation closure
Boeing 737-MAX analog: staged recoveries on each stream close. Position-sizing: lower conviction than the original framing.
Counterargument The Feb thesis was the wrong thesis. SMCI faces corporate-indictment risk in the 2nd probe, a securities class action with real damages potential, an ongoing SEC stream, and a co-founder criminal trial. The discount may widen, not close.
Response Each stream is scoped: DOJ already chose individuals over the corporation; 2nd probe is internal, not regulatory; class damages capped by class period (Apr 30 '24–Mar 19 '26). SMCI ~12–16x EV/EBITDA vs peer ~21x ≈ ~30% discount; staged closures historically recover 50–100% of similar discounts in 18–24 mo.
Thesis 3 falsified if: (a) DOJ superseding indictment names SMCI as corporate defendant; (b) SEC enforcement filed vs SMCI or current officers; (c) BDO issues qualified/adverse opinion on FY26 10-K; (d) class action survives MTD with significant claims intact.

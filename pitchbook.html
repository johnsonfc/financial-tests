Steelmanned counterargument A single quarter proves nothing structurally. Q3's 10.1% NG GM is below the FY23 18% peak and barely above FY24's 14% — the multi-year arc is down. Application-layer integrators equilibrate sub-10% GM; SMCI is converging to that, not breaking out.
Sources: SMCI 8-K guide range; historical margin trajectory — Chen/Liu Feb 18 2026; DCBBS non-disclosure noted in filings.

Response Tree

Residual risk acknowledgment Two branches partially hold. (1) The multi-year trend IS down; the bull case requires accepting ~10% application-layer equilibrium, not facilitation multiples — anyone modeling SMCI as facilitation will be disappointed. (2) DCBBS-specific margin is not filed, so ">20%" rests on management credibility — which, post-2024 EY resignation and 2026 indictment, deserves discount. What closes them: SMCI separately disclosing DCBBS revenue/margin in a future 10-K, or an analyst bottom-up triangulation. Until then: transitory cost-reversal = STRONG, structural-mix = MODERATE.
